## Your task

Before any code is written, conduct a structured interview to ensure we have clarity on what we're building. Walk through the following questions **one at a time**, waiting for my response before moving to the next:

1. **What is the core problem this solves?** — What pain point or gap exists today?
2. **Who is this for?** — Who are the users or stakeholders that benefit?
3. **What does success look like?** — How will we know this worked? What are the acceptance criteria?
4. **What should this NOT do?** — What's explicitly out of scope? What traps should we avoid?

After I've answered all four questions, **summarize everything back to me** in a concise brief:

- **Problem**: ...
- **Audience**: ...
- **Success criteria**: ...
- **Non-goals**: ...

Ask me to confirm the summary is accurate before we proceed. Do not write any code until I confirm.
